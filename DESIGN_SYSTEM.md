## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the understated luxury and tactile warmth of a bespoke travel journal merged with intuitive, cutting-edge AI capability. Built for discerning travelers planning complex itineraries, it cultivates an emotional atmosphere of serenity, clarity, and thoughtful discovery. Rather than relying on hyper-saturated gradients or frantic neon accents, the aesthetic leans into calm minimalism paired with warm, tactile elegance. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Cream paper tones and sun-drenched earth pigments evoke physical linen journals, architectural limestone, and warm soil.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke travel literature and vintage dispatch guides, balanced by clean geometric sans-serif UI typography.
- **Breathing Space:** Generous negative space reduces cognitive overload during multi-day trip coordination, allowing itinerary milestones and rich destination media to breathe effortlessly.
- **Tactile Softness:** Pill silhouettes, smooth continuous curves, and faint warm ambient light recreate the feeling of thumbing through luxury stationery.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an earthy, organic continuum that shifts gracefully between soft limestone paper and rich roasted espresso:

- **Primary (`#6e4f32` - Deep Umber):** Serves as the primary anchor for core calls-to-action, active icons, and high-emphasis body text, ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against cream substrates.
- **Secondary (`#8c6e52` - Warm Mocha):** Deployed for subtle action hover states, active tab highlights, secondary buttons, and focus outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#a28973` - Sunlit Clay):** Allocated to metadata icons, badge backgrounds, pill tag text, and timeline connectors.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#bba591` - Light Taupe):** Used at 40%–70% opacity for delicate hairline borders, horizontal rules, and timeline guides without fragmenting the canvas.
- **Surface Tint (`#d9c9ba` - Soft Sand):** Applied to muted input fields, secondary chip backgrounds, and resting container surfaces.
- **Base Canvas (`#f7f2ec` - Warm Cream):** The full-viewport canvas background, eliminating stark sterile white glare.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#ffffff` - Crisp Linen):** Exclusively reserved for floating card containers, active popovers, and elevated modal overlays to establish clean spatial depth.

## Typography

The type system leverages deliberate tension between literary romanticism and contemporary pragmatic UI design. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display`, bringing an editorial, storied cadence to travel destinations, trip names, and hero banners. It should never be used in all-caps or tightly condensed blocks.
- **Body & Interface:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, offering crisp geometry, broad apertures, and organic warmth that matches the rounded visual tone. 
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Reserve serif headlines for contextual headings and narrative AI itinerary summaries. Keep operational cues, timeline timestamps, metadata badges, and form labels strictly within `Plus Jakarta Sans` for rapid scanning and legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid system capped at a maximum inner content container of 1320px for desktop screens to preserve comfortable reading lengths:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, 48px (`margin`) outer screen margin, and 24px (`gutter`) column gutters. Itinerary sidebars lock to a 4-column span, leaving 8 columns for dynamic itinerary timeline flow and map views.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Dual-pane flows collapse into stacked tab views.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with 20px (`margin-mobile`) margins and 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Floating quick-action bars sit anchored to the lower safe area.

Spatial rhythm follows a strict 4px/8px incremental cadence. Section separation prioritizes ample empty room (`space-xl` and higher) over heavy graphic dividers to sustain the airy travel journal aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation avoids artificial drop shadows and cold grey tones, relying instead on warm diffused luminescence:

- **Ambient Elevation:** Shadows feature an extended blur radius tinted directly with warm deep brown: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(110, 79, 50, 0.08)`. For hovering cards or interactive itinerary blocks, the shadow elevates slightly to `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(110, 79, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#f7f2ec`. Content groups sit inside `#ffffff` surfaces bordered by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(187, 165, 145, 0.35)`).
- **Overlays & Modals:** Dialog backdrops use an ambient blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with a soft earthen wash (`rgba(247, 242, 236, 0.8)`), preserving underlying context without visually cluttering the view.

## Shapes

The design system uses a pronounced, pill-inspired shape language that imparts softness, comfort, and safety:

- **Buttons & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`), delivering a tactile, pebble-like quality when clicked or tapped.
- **Cards & Modal Sheets:** Formed with large radii (20px to 24px), harmonizing with the fluid geometry of the typography.
- **Input Fields & Textareas:** Styled with a 16px corner radius, balancing structured data entry with visual softness.
- **Image Containers:** Media clips and destination photography always inherit a minimum of 16px border-radius to avoid harsh orthogonal intersections against the soft palette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#6e4f32`, text `#ffffff`, border radius `9999px`, padding `12px 24px`. Hover state shifts background to `#8c6e52` with smooth 200ms ease transition. Active state scales down slightly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, hairline border `1px solid #bba591`, text `#6e4f32`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(217, 201, 186, 0.25)`.
- **Floating AI Trigger:** Floating prompt button with subtle warm ambient shadow, `#6e4f32` fill, and an internal delicate sparkle icon in warm clay (`#a28973`).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface filled with soft sand wash (`rgba(217, 201, 186, 0.3)`), border `1px solid rgba(187, 165, 145, 0.6)`, text `#6e4f32`, placeholder `#a28973`.
- Corner radius is `16px`, height `48px`, padding `0 18px`.
- On focus, input surface turns pure `#ffffff`, outline transitions to an outer mocha ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(140, 110, 82, 0.25)`, and border becomes `#8c6e52`.

### Cards & Itinerary Containers
- Surface: `#ffffff` for floating itinerary cards; subtle tint (`#f0e9e1`) for secondary informational containers.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(187, 165, 145, 0.35)`.
- Shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(110, 79, 50, 0.08)`.
- Radius: `20px` to `24px` with internal padding of `24px`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Fully rounded (`9999px`), padding `6px 14px`.
- Unselected: Background `rgba(217, 201, 186, 0.35)`, text `#6e4f32`, no border.
- Selected: Background `#6e4f32`, text `#ffffff`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: 20px rounded rectangle (radius `6px`), border `1.5px solid #bba591`. When checked, background fills with `#6e4f32` and checkmark renders in pure `#ffffff`.
- Radio: 20px circle with a centered `#6e4f32` dot on selection.

### Custom Component: Daily Timeline Milestone
- Vertical timeline thread colored `#bba591` (2px width, dashed or continuous).
- Milestone nodes: Circular pill badges (`32px` diameter) in `#f7f2ec` with an inner ring of `#8c6e52`.
- AI Recommendation callout: Highlighted with an ivory-tinted card, delicate warm gold border, and Playfair Display title.
