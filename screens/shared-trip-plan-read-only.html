1
Panjim Historic Quarter
Morning · 10:30 AM • 2.5h
Culture & Walk

Stroll through Fontainhas & Heritage Bakeries

Explore quiet, sun-drenched alleys flanked by 18th-century Portuguese bungalows, azulejo ceramic tiles, and warm Bebinca fresh from the wood-fired ovens of 31st January Bakery.

Cost: ₹350 (free walk)
Footwear: walking sandals
Aarav's Tip

Golden photo window is 10:00–11:15 AM before the heat builds. Grab fresh poi bread early!

2
Mum's Kitchen · Panjim
Afternoon · 02:00 PM • 1.5h
Culinary Heritage

Spice Luncheon at Mum's Kitchen

Celebrated preservation kitchen dedicated to forgotten Christian and Hindu Goan ancestral recipes. Savor authentic Meen Koddi, Kokum Solkadhi, and spicy Crab Xec Xec.

₹1,200 for two Reservation recommended

"Book patio table under the frangipani!"

3
Reis Magos Ramparts
Golden Hour · 05:00 PM • 2.0h
Scenic & Fort

Golden Hour Walk at Reis Magos Fort

Climb the restored red laterite fortress ramparts built in 1551. Enjoy panoramic views where the Mandovi River embraces the Arabian Sea as wooden fishing dhows glide home.

₹100 entry per person Closes at 6:00 PM
